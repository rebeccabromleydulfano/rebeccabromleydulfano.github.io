I am an MD candidate at the Stanford School of Medicine and received a PhD in health policy from Harvard University in 2026. I am currently applying to internal medicine residency programs.

My research focuses on health care delivery and equity. I study how underlying drivers of racial inequities in access to treatment, how changes in health system ownership affect access to specialty care, and changes in national trends in behavioral health integration into primary care. I also have a background in climate and health research and advocacy.

I received a BS with honors and a co-terminal MS in biology from Stanford University in 2018.
