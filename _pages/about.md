I am an MD candidate at the Stanford School of Medicine and received a PhD in health policy from Harvard University in 2026. I am currently applying to internal medicine residency programs, hoping to build a career that combines clinical care with research on how our health system works, and for whom.

My research focuses on health care delivery and equity. I study what drives racial inequities in access to treatment, including outpatient COVID-19 therapy; how the acquisition of safety-net hospitals by health systems changes access to cancer care; and how behavioral health has been integrated into primary care across the US. Together, these projects ask how the way care is organized affects who can get it.

I also have a background in climate and health research and advocacy. I co-founded Stanford Climate and Health, worked with Santa Clara County public health and sustainability offices on various climate equity planning projects, and helped co-organize two regional symposia on climate, health, and equity.

I received a BS with honors and a co-terminal MS in biology from Stanford University in 2018.
